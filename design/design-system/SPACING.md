# Spacing, radius, elevation, sizes (Design System v1.0)

- **Grid:** 4 pt. Tokens `xs 4 · sm 8 · md 12 · lg 16 · xl 20 · 2xl 24 · 3xl 32`.
- **Screen gutter** 16 · **gap between cards** 12 · **card padding** 16.
- **Radius:** `sm 8` (chips, inputs) · `md 12` (icon tiles) · `lg 16` (cards) · `xl 24` (hero card) · `full` (avatars, badges, round punch button).
- **Elevation:** `card` = `0 2px 8px rgba(65,48,204,.06)` + 1 px `border`; `raised` = `0 8px 24px rgba(65,48,204,.18)` (hero card, round punch button only).
- **Touch targets** ≥ 48 × 48 dp; punch controls ≥ 64 dp.
- **Safe areas:** top (notch / Dynamic Island) and bottom (home indicator) on every screen.
- **Frames:** small Android 360 × 640 · standard **390 × 844** (design frame used for mockups) · large 430 × 932. Portrait only.
- **No-scroll rule for home:** hero card + main action + first row of shortcuts must fit on 390 × 844 without scrolling.
